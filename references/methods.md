# Choose procedures by the failure they address

Use an inspectable intent record and flexible execution. [AI Chains](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~sherryw/assets/pubs/2022-chain.pdf)
supports separating operations that need different controls in studied tasks;
more stages can still add cost and cascade errors.

Check source faithfulness separately from external truth. [Maynez et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.173/)
shows why fluent summaries and word overlap do not establish fidelity. Retrieve
relevant personal examples for the current task, following the component-level
results of [LaMP](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.399/). Neither validates
recovering every unspoken owner intention.

Critique against original sources and permitted observations. [CRITIC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11738)
uses tool feedback; [intrinsic self-correction research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798)
cautions against treating repeated reflection as independent verification.

Consider relevance and interruption cost separately. [Iqbal and Bailey](https://interruptions.net/literature/Iqbal-CHI08.pdf)
supports breakpoint-sensitive notification in studied tasks, without proving
an owner-specific attention model or improved overall task resumption time.
[Horvitz](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/chi99horvitz.pdf)
offers mixed-initiative decision criteria under uncertainty.

Use comparable completed work when forecasting remaining effort. [Reference
class forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.3642) informs this adaptation;
infrastructure overruns are not personal coding-time calibration. No fixed
checkpoint interval or alert score is established as optimal for this owner.

These are component findings and practical adaptations. Evaluate the actual
artifact, timing and owner correction burden before claiming improvement.

For communication, [Clark and Brennan](https://web.stanford.edu/~clark/1990s/Clark%2C%20H.H.%20_%20Brennan%2C%20S.E.%20_Grounding%20in%20communication_%201991.pdf)
frames understanding as purpose-specific coordination with combined effort.
[Human-AI interaction guidelines](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf)
support contextual relevance, easy correction and dismissal in reviewed products.
Neither establishes a universal owner model.

Use [RST's author account](https://www.sfu.ca/rst/01intro/intro.html) as a private
check of cause, condition, purpose, evidence and optional background. Relations
can be ambiguous and extended; preserve novel meaning rather than forcing a
fixed taxonomy. Historical symbolic frameworks can inform interpretation when
the owner's context supports them, without classifying a personality or
imagining an unconscious motive.
