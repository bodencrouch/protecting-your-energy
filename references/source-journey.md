# Follow the source before naming the pattern

Begin with the owner's present question and actual available sources. An existing
profile, popular theory, fixed skill taxonomy or previous synthesis can influence
what gets noticed. For broad rediscovery, investigators first read originals
without those expected conclusions. Retain prior work as a versioned candidate
to compare after the new source account is available.

## Preserve the journey

Inventory source family, actual dates, snapshots, known exclusions and available
originals. Distinguish inventory/scanning, model reading and contextual hydration.
Include newer snapshots and inbox sources; canonical conversation Parquet can
lag an event stream. A named export directory is not the full available period.

Read broadly before choosing a few threads deeply. Follow the message's own
references, questions, repeated scenarios, changed proposals and consequential
replies. Record the reason for the next lead, including a semantic connection
whose wording differs. Keep a surprising singular idea as well as recurrence.
Success, pleasure, experimentation and practical design belong in the record.

An episode record can retain its original sources/IDs, dates, participants,
trigger, owner explanation, desired experience, questions, actual responses,
iterations and available outcome. Quote attribution is distinct from posting.
Retellings, copied AI prose and duplicate snapshots keep their source roles.

For AI histories, inspect the conversation type and message author/role before
printing matching text. Memory/profile exports, scaffolding, assistant answers
and quoted or pasted generated prose can resemble owner statements. A user-role
wrapper alone does not establish original authorship or adoption of every claim.
Keep authored requests as design evidence, distinguish embedded claims, and
follow their original discussion when needed. Record accidental exposure rather
than claiming a clean independent pass after it happened.

A frontier record retains the unanswered reference, source that raised it,
possible next originals and what information could clarify it. Failed retrieval
does not convert that unknown into absence. Preserve the frontier across sessions.

## Interpret without rewriting the person

Write the source account in the owner's terms before proposing a reusable method.
Keep observed sequence, stated reason, inferred connection and proposed design
separate in the private record. Locate the reason a principle or debate mattered:
what question it answered, earlier experience, conditions and desired result.
Do not automatically minimize complex reasons because an explanation is simpler,
or replace persistence with a trait or diagnosis. Charitable reconstruction can
preserve a contested idea without pretending every external assertion is proved.

Compare distinct scenarios for shared purpose or causal mechanism, changed
conditions and revisions. Similar wording alone is insufficient; different
wording can carry the same method. Keep plausible alternatives and disagreements
until context resolves them. Record how a new source changed the interpretation.
Agent agreement is not the resolution criterion.

Then express a candidate method in the form that fits its actual source. The
goal/reason/conditions/operation/result scaffold is optional, not a requirement
that every idea fit one schema. Accurate established terms may clarify a concept
after its meaning is understood. Novel relationships remain expressible.

## Carry the understanding into work

Apply only the relevant supported method to the current requested result. Keep
the owner's intended experience and meaningful choices; handle incidental work.
The output should be the useful artifact, not an analysis the owner must operate.
Retain original provenance, interpretation alternatives, current correction and
the next unresolved link privately. Existing `energy_state.py` can version
episode, interpretation and frontier records alongside methods and tasks.

Evaluate new-cue retrieval, material meaning, unsupported additions, correction
retention and resumption with actual source-derived requests. Record what was
really exercised, including user steps and concrete remaining blockers. A review
score, a tool response or a readable draft does not measure owner benefit.
Allow supported work to continue while remaining source investigation proceeds.

## Method grounding

[Braun and Clarke's process account](https://www.thematicanalysis.net/doing-reflexive-ta/)
supports familiarisation, rereading and iterative development/review of patterns.
[Reflexis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15445) explores reflection, traceable analytical
evolution and productive interpretive disagreement. Borrow these components;
this workflow is an engineering adaptation, not a claim to perform a complete
qualitative study or to eliminate analyst influence. Concrete factual attribution
checks and usable-outcome checks remain separate from interpretive agreement.
