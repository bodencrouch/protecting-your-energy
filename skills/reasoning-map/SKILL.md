# Recover the reasoning that matters now

Treat rough wording, deliberate posting, repeated explanation, passionate
emphasis and iteration as cues to invested reasoning. Recover what the owner
was trying to accomplish, why it mattered and what would make the effort useful.
Do not reduce these cues to word frequency or a personality label.

## Recover before advising

Start from the current goal, audience, decision and source period the owner
identifies. Use the current message to locate relevant original episodes.
Search complementary semantic facets and other relationships or dates. Read
through referents, replies, corrections and outcomes. Seek contrary settings
before making a method durable. Existing implementations and summaries are
leads, not the definition of the problem.

Deduplicate export snapshots by stable source/message identity. A deliberate
repost or return to an idea in a different setting is a distinct investment
event, while the underlying idea may remain the same. Distinguish owner-created
text, quoted/copied work, exploration, endorsement and later rejection. Strong
emphasis does not establish factual truth, current priority or agreement.
Use a distinct investment event to investigate a continuation, needed repair
or reusable method. Record what changed, what effort recurred and which useful
operation follows under the present conditions. Do not stop at describing the
owner as invested or recurring; connect the cue to the current artifact.

Trace relations between episodes: continuation, elaboration, revision, contrast,
shared mechanism, or only topic overlap. Before linking pieces, explain their
shared purpose or causal relationship privately. Preserve alternatives when
that relationship is unresolved. Recover missing premises and subject/pronoun
corrections; do not fill an actual knowledge gap with a confident invention.

## Build a revisable method

For each useful pattern, retain:

`goal → causal reason → applicable conditions → useful operation → result`

Also retain counterconditions, dated corrections, original sources, uncertainty
and the observed outcome. Separate the owner's stated problem from the proposed
operational adaptation. A supplied tool or identity shorthand can be a means to
a deeper goal. Keep that distinction without deleting the original proposal.

Use [continuity](../../references/continuity.md) for the private method record.
The model is a contextual aid, not independent evidence about the owner. Load
only the methods and original examples relevant to the present task. Later
corrections revise applicability; preserve prior versions. Do not promote an
inferred rule into unrelated global memory or claim complete psychological
understanding from a bounded archive pass.

Choose an established term when it represents the actual mechanism, relation or
goal accurately. Verify uncertain concepts through primary sources. Preserve
novel meaning that a familiar category would erase. Concision should retain the
links necessary to understand the claim; optional depth can be offered at the
appropriate moment. Keep terminology and causal inference distinct from
scientific validation of a claim.

## Apply the pattern

Compare the present situation with the method's goal, conditions, latest
correction and counterconditions. Select the smallest useful operation within
the request: develop the idea, recover a missing connection, draft the message,
choose the next result, preserve a checkpoint, or verify a new opportunity.
Preserve meaningful ambition and creative choices. When a particular means or
participant is unavailable, carry the underlying goal toward an authorized
independent result rather than repeating the same unsuccessful route.

Deliver the artifact appropriate to the audience. Keep the pattern ledger,
prompt mechanics and source investigation privately available. Record what the
owner corrected and how the next invocation should change within that scope.
Evaluate source support, preserved meaning, usefulness and effort to resume;
fluent writing or agent agreement does not establish those outcomes.
