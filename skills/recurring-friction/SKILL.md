# Remove a repeated obstacle

Begin with the result the owner wants and the effort repeatedly getting in its
way. Retrieve distinct episodes, attempted remedies, corrections and outcomes.
Use [archive access](../../references/archive-access.md) and `reasoning-map`.
Prioritize the sustained work and period the owner identifies. Search related
causes and methods across other contexts; a shared project name or symptom does
not establish a shared cause. Preserve separate occurrences while deduplicating
exports, generated retellings and replayed transcripts.

Recover the causal chain: intended result, triggering condition, missing context
or capability, owner effort, attempted response, outcome and counterexample.
An implicit design in an ordinary complaint or iteration deserves investigation.
Preserve meaningful creative decisions and challenges. Carry the incidental
mechanics when that is the desired experience; do not remove valued work merely
because it takes effort.

Choose the smallest reusable intervention that addresses the evidenced cause:
an applicable remembered reason, clearer expression, a reliable tool route,
an automatic checkpoint, a reusable implementation or a scheduled change check.
Implement reversible work within the authorized scope. Existing implementations
and conventional labels may help, but must not define the problem. A useful new
design can extend beyond the historical mechanism; retain its source basis and
identify the continuation privately.

Make the intervention concrete: input, trigger, permitted action, useful result,
how a correction changes it and when it stops or falls back. Reuse active jobs
and supported scheduling. Respect project-specific constraints and actual
publication, sending and destructive-action boundaries. The current request can
already authorize automation; do not restart a permission questionnaire.

For automatic updates, recover what decision the owner could make differently.
Check on a useful cadence; notify for a meaningful new result, removed blocker,
actionable opening or recurring failure that needs attention. Batch nonurgent
items and preserve an unchanged finding without repeatedly alerting. Use verified
deadlines to justify urgency. A schedule cannot observe live availability unless
a reliable attention signal is actually supplied.

Record the method, applicability, evidence, trigger and observed outcome in
[continuity](../../references/continuity.md). Evaluate an actual recurrence and
a countercondition when available: does it remove owner steps, preserve the
requested result and recover from correction? A working schedule or helper is
component evidence; sustained time saved remains unmeasured until observed.

Return the usable intervention and one ordinary way to invoke or correct it.
Keep the recurrence analysis behind the result unless an evaluation is requested.
