# Release series

Protecting Your Energy is released as a sequence of reviewed boundaries:

1. private foundation;
2. private dogfood and evaluation;
3. sponsor preview when access terms are ready;
4. public documentation and portfolio narrative;
5. results-led brand and portfolio distribution: GitHub narrative, owned-site case study, Medium or publication draft, developer-community adaptation, and sponsor update;
6. public core or general release after a separate owner decision.

Every stage shares one revision, claim ledger, privacy review, and rollback
reference. The marketing surface is an integration layer over the reviewed
release, not an independent source of truth.

For each stage, prepare:

- a canonical GitHub announcement;
- an owned-site case study;
- a Medium article or publication submission where appropriate;
- a developer-community adaptation such as DEV;
- a short sponsor or changelog update when relevant.

Each item gets its own audience, title, call to action, links, and evidence
boundary. Drafts may be generated in parallel, but an owner approves claims
and publication individually. The plugin never posts private conversation
material, raw personal evidence, sponsor-only implementation details, or
unreleased source.
