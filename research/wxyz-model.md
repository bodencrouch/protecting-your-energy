# WXYZ model

Protecting Your Energy studies how raw human signals become avoidable communication friction, then turns the smallest supported correction into a protected method and a measurable public result.

```text
W: raw human signal
  -> X: visible interaction friction
  -> Y: protected method that resolves it
  -> Z: verified result and earned reputation
```

W is not a diagnosis or judgment. It includes ordinary emotion, impatience, indirect language, posturing, and uncertainty. X is the observable pattern that makes collaboration costly: repeated clarification, dropped dependencies, premature stopping, vague status, or failure to infer an obvious next step.

Y is internal intellectual property. Protect it through abstraction, modular disclosure, access boundaries, and honest descriptions of what has been validated. Avoiding unnecessary reproduction of the method in public artifacts does not justify hiding risks or fabricating novelty.

Z is the external promise: clearer communication, completed work, trustworthy status, useful documentation, and reputation earned from evidence. Public releases lead with Z, explain only the Y needed to make the result credible, and keep private source material out of the release.

## Product rule

The plugin never activates a WXYZ-derived preference by default. It may show the observed X, evidence, a candidate Y-level intervention, scope, and expected Z-level benefit. The user chooses whether it applies for the response, task, project, or future work.

## Release rule

Each staged release delivers a useful Z-level artifact: a working integration, reproducible evaluation, or clearly bounded result. Split Y into coherent modules with stable interfaces and evidence boundaries. Do not release arbitrary fragments solely to create suspense.
